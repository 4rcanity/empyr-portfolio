Redesign Ledger and UNO tables, add RANGE-07 aim trainer

The Ledger board was unreadable along the top edge: a leftover 180-degree
rotation on the top row's face flipped every name upside down, on top of the
column-reverse that already moves the colour band to the inner edge. Removing
it leaves the top row upright, and the side columns were re-derived rather
than assumed. Tiles now carry a real type hierarchy, players are struck brass
seals that travel between squares, and trading and folding have permanent
homes in the rail instead of being buried in modals. The flat cream sheet is
now a late-night counting-house table.

UNO's flat sidebar of player pods is replaced by a ring: seats sit on an
ellipse in true turn order from you and re-order when a Reverse lands, each
with a card fan angled to its position. The felt is tipped on a perspective
while anything that must stay readable is barely tilted at all. Every FxKind
the server already emitted now has its own treatment rather than collapsing
into one text flash.

RANGE-07 is a new client-only aim trainer: seven drills, sensitivity
conversion built on real per-game yaw constants, pointer lock with a cursor
fallback for browsers that refuse it, and a post-run report whose numbers are
asserted by a headless harness rather than merely rendered.
